Why it matters

Growth hides cost creep.

When sales rise every year, a cost line rising twice as fast still looks normal in the books. Nobody flags it, because the rupee amount grew for everyone.

In our sample manufacturer, revenue grew 7.2% a year. Freight grew 14% and power 12.5%. Along with materials and staff costs, that drift took 5.4 points off EBITDA margin in three years.

DriftLine measures every cost line against revenue, year by year, so the creep is visible the day you upload.

How it works

From statements to a cost diagnosis in three steps.

Step 1

Upload your statements

A results PDF or annual report, or your P&L and balance sheet as Excel or CSV. Schedule III, GAAP and plain-English line names are recognised automatically.

Step 2

See where costs drifted

Each cost line is compared with its best year as a share of revenue. You see the gap in rupees, and choose how much of it you think is realistic to recover.

Step 3

Know what to check next

Every finding lists what the statements can't show, the records to pull, and the usual fixes. Ask Plop, the built-in analyst, anything about your numbers.

Straight talk

What annual statements can't tell you, and what we do about it.

Plenty of tools turn ratios into confident advice. DriftLine tells you where to look, and is honest about what the numbers alone can't prove.

  • Price versus volume. A rising material cost could be commodity prices, product mix or waste. We list the records that separate them.
  • Vendors and contracts. Statements show totals, not suppliers. Renegotiation needs your purchase data.
  • Costs you shouldn't cut. Marketing, maintenance and people come with a warning, because cutting them can cost next year's revenue.
  • Your peers. Today we compare you with your own best year. Industry benchmarks are on our roadmap.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

What format do my statements need to be in?

Upload the company's results PDF or annual report, and DriftLine finds the full-year profit and loss statement and balance sheet in it. You check the numbers before relying on the report. Or use Excel or CSV with one row per line item and one column per financial year. Scanned PDFs can't be read yet. Three or more years gives the most reliable picture.

What does "gap to best year" mean?

For each cost, we find the year it was lowest as a share of revenue. The gap is what you'd save today if it were back at that share. It's a ceiling, not a forecast, which is why you set how much of it you think is achievable.

Does this replace my CA?

No. DriftLine shows where costs have drifted and what to check. Your CA or finance team brings the context: one-off items, contracts and decisions behind the numbers. Many firms use it to start that conversation.

Can I trust the numbers?

The calculations are simple and shown on screen, so you can check any figure against your statements. The limits are in the data: annual statements can't separate price from volume, so every finding tells you which records would confirm it.
