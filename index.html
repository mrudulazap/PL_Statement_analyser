Why it matters

Growth hides cost creep.

When sales rise every year, a cost growing twice as fast still looks normal in the books. Nobody flags it, because every rupee figure went up.

Say revenue grows 7% a year while freight grows 14% and power 12%. Each year looks fine on its own, but over three years that kind of drift can take several points off your margin.

DriftLine measures every cost line against revenue, year by year, so the creep is visible the moment you upload.

Honest by design

What annual statements can't tell you, and what DriftLine does about it.

Plenty of tools turn ratios into confident advice. DriftLine shows you where to look, and is clear about what the numbers alone can't prove.

  • Price versus volumeA rising material cost could be commodity prices, product mix or waste. Each finding lists the records that separate them.
  • Vendors and contractsStatements show totals, not suppliers. Renegotiation needs your purchase data, and DriftLine says which.
  • Costs you shouldn't cutMarketing, maintenance and people come with a warning, because cutting them can cost next year's revenue.
  • Your peersToday DriftLine compares you with your own best year. Industry comparisons are on the roadmap.

FAQ

Questions owners ask.

DriftLine shows its working on screen, so you can check every figure against your statements.

What format do my statements need to be in?

Upload the company's results PDF or annual report, and DriftLine finds the full-year profit and loss statement and balance sheet in it. You check the numbers before relying on the report. Or use Excel or CSV with one row per line item and one column per financial year. Scanned PDFs can't be read yet. Three or more years gives the most reliable picture.

What does "gap to best year" mean?

For each cost, DriftLine finds the year it was lowest as a share of revenue. The gap is what you'd save today if it were back at that share. It's a ceiling, not a forecast, which is why you set how much of it you think is achievable.

Does this replace my CA?

No. DriftLine shows where costs have drifted and what to check. Your CA or finance team brings the context: one-off items, contracts and the decisions behind the numbers. Many firms use it to start that conversation.

Can I trust the numbers?

The calculations are simple and shown on screen, so you can check any figure against your statements. The limits are in the data: annual statements can't separate price from volume, so every finding tells you which records would confirm it.

Is it really free?

Yes, for every business. There's no trial, no sign-up and no card. The analysis runs in your own browser, so it costs very little to run. Plop has a fair-use limit so it stays free for everyone.
